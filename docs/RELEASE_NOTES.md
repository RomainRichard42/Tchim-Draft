## English — Updated Worlds games and draft statistics

25 October 2026

- **Review 38 Worlds games**, up from 8: 22 Play-In and 16 Main Event games, with available results through 24 October. Their picks, bans, matchups and synergies enrich the pro statistics.
- **Prepare your choices on patch 16.21.** Master+ statistics are refreshed for patches 16.21, 16.20 and 16.19. Pro data retains its own three latest available patches: 16.20, 16.18 and 16.17.
- **Browse updated kits for all 173 champions.** Bilingual descriptions, patch 16.21 ability parameters and changes from the three latest patches are refreshed.
- **A retrained model with more complete data.** Verified dates, teams and drafts feed a new training run. The latest pro patch remains reserved for evaluation; a candidate that fails the validation thresholds stays inactive. Small samples do not become certainties.

Shared statistics and champion kits sync after sign-in. This update never starts website collection on users’ computers. Scores remain comparison aids, not validated win probabilities.
